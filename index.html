MSc dissertation, University of Sheffield, United Kingdom, 2026

Knowledge distillation for BEVFusion

BEVFusion merges camera images and LiDAR into one bird's-eye-view map to find cars, people and obstacles in 3D. It is accurate and heavy. I distilled it into a compact student built from YOLO and PointPillars, and benchmarked it on nuScenes.

Agentic LLM system

Race Engineer Agent

An LLM that plays Formula 1 race engineer. It reads FastF1 timing data, calls tools to compare pace, model tyre wear and simulate pit windows, then decides what to tell the driver, including when to say nothing.

  • Runs on Gemini, or locally on Qwen 3 14B
  • Guardrails against repeated messages and bad pit calls
  • Instrumented end to end and covered by tests
  • Python
  • FastF1
  • Ollama
  • OpenTelemetry
